# Playtest review, 3 Oct 2026: 5.5 / 10, and what's been done

A full playtest scored like a Rockstar / EA greenlight review:
- **Scores:** concept 7, main character 7, other people 5, vehicles 4, city art 4, gameplay 2, interface 6, speed / tech 8.
- **Verdict:** a convincing true-scale Times Square with nothing to *do*.

**Product rule (from the same day):** the only money in the game is for buying property or billboards. Game loops reward with stars, trips, streaks and time, never cash or items.

## Fixed

| Issue found | Fix | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Planes "crashed" the moment they left Midtown (any height): the Statue flight was impossible | Outside Midtown the plane only checks harbour towers, the Statue and the water; the world's edge turns you back gently | `flight.js` |
| People walked through cars and through you | People hurry out of a moving car's path, are pushed out of any car body, and make room for you. Traffic cars stop for them, and your car brakes for them | `avatar.js` `Npcs.avoid`, `traffic.js` via `npcs.near`, `ride.js` `people` |
| Cars could drive through the car-free Broadway / 7th Ave plazas | The plazas stop cars at the edge (bollards), with "Pedestrian plaza: cars can't drive here" | `ride.js` `drive()` |
| A 49 mph crash just stopped, with no feedback | A thump plus crunch sound (scaled by speed), a "Crash" caption, a camera kick in both views, a bounce back and a short stall | `ride.js`, `audio.js` `crash()` |
| No shadows: people and cars floated | Soft contact shadows under every person (they stay on the ground when you jump) and every car | `shadow.js` (stacked ellipses) |
| Empty side streets | Traffic 45 → 80 cars on desktop, spawned 40–220 m from you (was 70–290). Far, unseen people reappear out of sight near you | `index.html` `trafficCount`, `traffic.js` `spawn`, `Npcs.recycle` |
| Horn captions with no car in sight | Horns only come from a real car within 120 m | `audio.js` `carNear` |
| The road showed the river (58 gaps in Midtown's street mesh, e.g. W 37th St) | An asphalt underlay 8 cm below street level across Midtown's land | `index.html` `groundUnderlay` |
| "Fly this plane" offered from the street | On foot it's only offered up on the flight deck, beside the plane | `flight.js` |
| An empty tooltip pill by the selected dock button | Tooltips keep their own text colour | `index.html` CSS |
| "Go" to your cab pickup jumped to an aerial camera (and threw an error) | On foot, Go walks you there; a point without a view flies to an overview | `nav.js` `wpfly` |
| Pale patches at the calves on jumps | Socks hidden inside trouser legs are removed (they poked through on bent knees) | `game_ready.py` |
| No gameplay loop | **Cab fares** (J or "Take fares" in a cab you drive): a passenger at a curb with a beacon, a real Midtown destination, a clock, a route on the minimap, a 1–5★ trip rating (late or bumpy rides lose stars), your driver rating and on-time streak saved. No money | `fares.js` |

**Not a bug:** the "black bar" after leaving the cab by the Intrepid is the Pier 86 deck seen edge-on.

**Measured after the fixes:** a steady 60 fps on the bowtie with 79 cars and 100 people.
