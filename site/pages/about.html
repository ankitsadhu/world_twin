About {{business.brand}}

{{business.brand}} builds real places as playable worlds in the browser. Our first world is Times Square and Midtown Manhattan at real scale: the streets, the theatres, the screens, the Hudson and the Statue of Liberty, with the real New York time of day.

The game is free to play and has no in-game money. The only thing anyone pays for is a permanent slot: a screen, a billboard or a building. That keeps the game fair for players and gives advertisers something simple and honest to buy.
