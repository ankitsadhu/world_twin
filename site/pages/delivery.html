Delivery Policy

Effective {{business.effective_date}}

Everything we sell is digital. Nothing is shipped, and there are no shipping costs.

What you receive

  1. Order confirmation by email as soon as your payment is confirmed. It contains your order number, the slot you bought and a link to upload your artwork.
  2. Artwork review: we review your artwork within {{business.artwork_review_days}} business days of receiving it. If it needs changes, we email you exactly what to change.
  3. Going live: once approved, your artwork is shown on your slot in the game within {{business.delivery_days}} business days. We email you when it is live.

If something is late

If your slot is not live within {{business.delivery_days}} business days of artwork approval, email {{business.support_email}} and we will fix it or refund you in full.

Proof of delivery

Your order confirmation, the "your slot is live" email and your slot's page in the game are your record of delivery. Keep your order confirmation.

When your year starts

Your 1-year term starts on the day your artwork goes live, not on the day you pay, so review time is never taken out of your year.

Changing your artwork later

You may replace your artwork at any time. New artwork is reviewed in the same way.
