What we collect

When you play the game

  • A random anonymous ID stored in your browser, with events such as "started a ride", how long a session lasted, your screen size, whether you use touch, a rough device class, and technical errors. It does not contain your name or email and we do not try to identify you from it.

When you buy a slot

  • Your name, email, billing country and the details you give us for your order and invoice.
  • Your payment is handled by Razorpay. We receive confirmation and a payment reference, not your card number.
  • The artwork and links you upload.

When you email us

  • Your email address and what you write.

Why we use it, and our legal basis

  • To run the game and keep it working, fix errors and measure performance: our legitimate interest.
  • To process and deliver your order, keep a record of who owns each slot, and meet tax and accounting rules: contract and legal obligation.
  • To answer you: legitimate interest or contract.

We do not sell your personal data and we do not use it for advertising profiles. We do not use third-party advertising or tracking cookies.

Cookies and local storage

The game stores an anonymous ID, your settings and your progress in your browser's local storage. The ID is used for the aggregate statistics described above. If you are in the EU or UK and prefer not to have it, you can clear site data in your browser at any time or tell us and we will delete the records linked to it.

Who we share data with

  • Razorpay, to take payment.
  • Google Cloud and Cloudflare, who host and deliver the site and the game.
  • Authorities, if the law requires.

Our servers and these providers may be outside your country, including in the United States and India. Where required we use standard contractual clauses or equivalent safeguards.

How long we keep it

  • Order records, including the ownership record of each slot, are kept for as long as the slot exists and as the law requires (the ownership record is permanent by design).
  • Game statistics are anonymous and kept in aggregate.
  • Emails to us are kept as long as needed to answer and for our records.

Your rights

California residents can ask what we hold and ask us to delete it; we do not sell or share personal data for advertising. To use any right, email {{business.support_email}}. We reply within 30 days.

Children

You must be 18 to buy a slot. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children through the shop.

Security

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS) and keep payment data with the payment provider. No system is perfectly secure; if there is a breach that affects you, we will tell you as the law requires.
