Refund and Cancellation Policy

Effective {{business.effective_date}}

All sales are final

A slot is a permanent, digital item that is yours alone once purchased. We do not offer refunds, returns or cancellations after a purchase is completed. Please read the slot description, the price and the Terms before you pay. You will be asked to confirm this at checkout.

What this means in practice

  • You cannot cancel an order once payment is confirmed.
  • We do not refund because you changed your mind, chose the wrong slot, or no longer need it.
  • If your artwork needs changes to meet the content rules, we will ask you to send a corrected version. That is not a reason for a refund.

When we do refund

  • We cannot deliver your slot: for example, it turns out the slot was already sold, or we are unable to activate it. You get a full refund to your original payment method.
  • You were charged incorrectly: a duplicate or wrong-amount charge is refunded in full once we confirm it.
  • The law requires it (see below).

Refunds we owe are issued to the original payment method within 7 business days of our confirmation; your bank may take longer to show it.

Your legal rights

Nothing in this policy limits rights you have under the law that cannot be excluded. If you are a consumer in the European Union or the United Kingdom, you normally have a 14-day right to cancel online purchases. For a digital item that we deliver straight away, this right is lost once delivery has begun with your express request and your acknowledgement that you lose it. At checkout you will be asked to confirm both. If you do not confirm, we will not start delivery until the 14 days have passed.
