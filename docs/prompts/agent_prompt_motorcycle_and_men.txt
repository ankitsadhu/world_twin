5. WHAT THE GAME DOES WITH YOUR FILES (so you can see why the above matters)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 - It loads the GLB and finds parts BY NAME (BIKE, STEER, WHEEL_FRONT, WHEEL_REAR, KICKSTAND, SEAT_ANCHOR, GRIP_*, FOOT_*, M_* materials).
 - It poses everything procedurally (steering, lean, kickstand, wheel spin): it does NOT play your clips, so the numbers in
   bike_info.json are the source of truth.
 - It tints M_Paint, M_Top, M_Dress, M_Denim, M_Jacket, M_Cap per person/bike (light base colours multiply).
 - It rebinds skinned accessories and the far LOD to the avatar skeleton by bone name.
 - It places every vehicle by the ground under its wheels (pavements are 16 cm above the road): the model's rest-state tyre
   bottoms at z = 0 is what makes wheels sit on the ground instead of in it.
 - Web budget: every extra megabyte is paid by every visitor. Keep to the budgets.
