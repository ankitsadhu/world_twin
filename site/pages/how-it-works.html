How it works

1. Choose a slot

Pick a screen, billboard, banner or building from the batch on sale.

2. Pay once

A single payment. No subscription, no renewal. All sales are final.

3. Send your artwork

You receive an order confirmation and a link to upload your image or short video.

4. We review it

We check it meets the content rules and fits the slot. If it needs changes we tell you what to fix.

5. It goes live

Your artwork appears in the game for every player. You get an email when it is live.

6. It stays yours

You can replace your artwork later. The slot stays yours for as long as the game is operated.
