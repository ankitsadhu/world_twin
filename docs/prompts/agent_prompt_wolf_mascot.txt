PROMPT: WOLF-HEADED MUSCULAR MAN, THE GAME MASCOT (game-ready on our shared rig)

GOAL
A mascot character for our real-scale Times Square web game (three.js): a muscular man's body with the head of a wolf.
Friendly-confident, not horror. Stylised-realistic: photoreal materials, believable anatomy, no cartoon proportions.
He walks, runs, rides our cruiser motorcycle and stands as a brand mascot beside billboards.

BODY (same standard as our muscular man)
- Height 1.85 m (the wolf head included). V-shaped athlete: chest ~1.12 m, waist ~0.82 m, shoulder:waist >= 1.45,
  thick neck with trapezius, rounded deltoids, defined pecs/lats/abs, upper arm 0.38-0.40 m, forearm 0.31 m, thigh 0.64 m, calf 0.41 m.
- Human hands and feet (hands with 5 fingers, no claws). Bare-armed: a fitted sleeveless dark-grey tank top, black straight
  jeans, boots, a simple leather wrist band. Keep clothes in light/neutral base colours so we can tint them per variant.
- Bare skin (arms, neck, hands) is covered in fine short grey-brown fur on a human-shaped body (see FUR); the palms are bare.

HEAD (the wolf)
- Grey-brown timber wolf: long muzzle, black nose, amber eyes, upright pointed ears, white muzzle/chin/chest-of-neck, dark saddle
  down the back of the neck, a thick fur ruff around the neck blending into the shoulders. Mouth closed, calm confident expression.
- The rig has NO jaw bone: model the mouth closed and static. Ears, ruff and fur are static (they follow the Head bone). No tail.
- Head size: ~1.45x a human head (mascot read), set on a human neck. Eyes level with a human eye line (about 1.72 m).

FUR (game budget!)
- Strand/shell fur is too heavy. Use alpha-cut fur CARDS on the head, ruff and forearms, plus a fur albedo/normal in the skin
  texture. Fur cards <= 8,000 triangles near, <= 2,000 far. Material M_Fur (alpha clip, double sided), light grey base so we can tint.

RIG AND POSES (identical to our existing characters)
- The SAME 19 Mixamo-named bones, same orientations, A-pose at rest (Pose_ArmsDown, Pose_HandOnHip). Exactly 19 bones: no extra
  bones, no Icosphere, no cameras/lights. Blender metres, Z up, FACES -Y, feet at z = 0, origin between the feet. No global export
  scale (export_scale 1.0). Bone lengths may differ: write them in character_info.json (thigh, shank, hip height, ankle height, height).
- Skinning must hold up in: arms down, riding pose (hands forward on grips), run (elbows 90 deg, arms pumping), arms overhead.
  No collapsing shoulders, no stretched neck/ruff, no fur cards poking through the collar.
- Also deliver ride_pose_wolf.glb (armature + action "Pose_Ride" only) for our cruiser: hips on SEAT_ANCHOR (0, +0.20, 0.77),
  GRIP_L/R (+-0.38, -0.22, 1.11), FOOT_L/R (+-0.23, -0.25, 0.30), slight forward lean, knees clear of the tank (thighs 0.64 m).

MATERIALS (exact names, Principled BSDF, image textures, light base colours: the game tints them)
M_Skin, M_Fur, M_Eye (amber, glossy), M_Nose, M_Top, M_Denim, M_Boots, M_Leather. Eyes and nose separate objects.

BUDGET (game-ready, no further processing by us)
- wolf_mascot.glb:     <= 35,000 triangles total, <= 1.0 MB, textures <= 1024 px, Draco.
- wolf_mascot_far.glb: <= 10,000 triangles, <= 450 KB, textures <= 512 px, same rig, binds to the same bone names.
- Clothes: seams welded (shared vertices, identical weights), no open duplicate-vertex seams (tears when legs swing).
  Skin hidden under clothes removed (keep a band at waist, neck, cuffs).

ACCESSORIES (optional, <= 300 KB each, skinned to the same 19 bones, light base colour): denim jacket fitted to his chest and arms,
baseball cap with ear holes (static, sits between the ears).

FILES
